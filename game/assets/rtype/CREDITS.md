https://opengameart.org/content/space-shooter-redux: each icon is drawn on a dark disc with a
coloured ring, in a row of 48x48 cells.

## Generated interface art

Made by the team with `scripts/generate_ui.py` (Pillow only, no third-party art):
`sprites/ui/logo.png`, `sprites/ui/buttons.png`, `sprites/ui/hud.png`, `backgrounds/menu_space.png`,
`backgrounds/stage2_interior.png` and `sounds/INTRO_CHIMES.wav`. Run the script again to rebuild them.
